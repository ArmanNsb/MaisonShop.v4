How it works

  1. 1

    Choose your celebration.

    Every Maison piece and service has a limited number of spots at its Fork Monday price, from a box of Persian cookies to a full Sofreh.

  2. 2

    Buy it as a credit.

    For custom services, you buy a credit at the special price. A $12,000 Destination Sofreh credit, for example, is yours for $9,500.

  3. 3

    Celebrate within the year.

    Book your celebration any time in the next 12 months, based on availability. If your final order comes to more than your credit, the difference is billed at our regular price.
